The immaterial suitcase

Use the VIM app to collect and submit field forms.

Open the app from your phone, install it on the Home screen and fill in forms even when the connection is unstable. Submissions are queued and synced as soon as possible.

Open app.vim.quivi.it How to install it
Quick access
app.vim.quivi.it

On mobile, tap the link. From a computer, scan this QR code with your phone camera.

01

Install the PWA on your Home screen

iPhone / iPad
  1. Open app.vim.quivi.it with Safari.
  2. Tap the share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Confirm with Add.
Android
  1. Open app.vim.quivi.it with Chrome.
  2. When it appears, tap Install app.
  3. If it does not appear, open the browser menu.
  4. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
02

Fill in and submit a form

Sign in

Enter the username and password provided by the VIM project.

Fill in

Open Fill in form, choose the language and complete the required fields.

Attach media

Record audio or video, take photos or upload files already on the device.

Submit

Tap Completed and choose automatic or manual sending. If you are offline, the form stays in the queue.

03

Reference screens

Sign in with the credentials assigned to you.

The Home screen contains the main actions and archive.

You can save a draft and continue later.

Record audio or video directly from the browser when the form asks for it.

Complete the form when all required information has been entered.

Send pending forms or keep them in the queue until the connection is available.

Before going into the field

  • Open the app at least once with an active connection.
  • Check that sign-in, microphone, camera and free storage work.
  • Send whenever you can: data remains on the device, but it depends on browser limits.
